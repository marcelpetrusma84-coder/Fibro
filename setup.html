A little help for your device

iPhone or iPad
  1. Open Fibro in Safari.
  2. Open the Share menu. If needed, open the browser's More menu first.
  3. Choose Add to Home Screen, keep Open as Web App enabled if shown, and tap Add.

On a new app installation you may need to sign in and restore your key with Face ID or your recovery code.

Android
  1. Open Fibro in Chrome or your usual browser.
  2. Open the browser menu and choose Install app or Add to Home screen.
  3. Confirm and open the new Fibro icon.
Windows, Mac or Linux
  1. In Chrome or Edge, use the browser's install icon or its app installation menu.
  2. On Safari for Mac, choose File → Add to Dock when available.
  3. You can also use the shortcut folder above without installing the app.

Where supported, hold or right-click the installed Fibro icon to find Home and Key recovery. Both links are also available from Fibro's Home menu. To make a folder on your phone's home screen, group the icons yourself using your phone's controls.

The folder contains public links and icons only. Your private key, password and recovery code stay out of it.

Key recovery restores your existing key. These shortcuts do not create or replace encryption keys.
